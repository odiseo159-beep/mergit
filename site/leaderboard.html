How to score.

01

Get test ETH

Free from faucet.giwa.io. It is testnet money and has no real value. Your wallet gets the GIWA Sepolia network when you connect on this site.

02

Claim your GitHub login

At mergit.xyz/claim. It links your login to your wallet, so the agent knows where to pay you. It also counts: +10.

03

Fund or ship

Fund one of the issues below, or pick a funded one and open a pull request with Closes #N. When it is merged with green CI, the agent pays and the points land.

  • Only payments signed by the Mergit agent count. That means a real pull request, merged by the repository's maintainer, with green CI.
  • A bounty scores only if it locked at least 0.0005 ETH. Paying yourself scores nothing. Neither does a bounty that is still open or was refunded.
  • Funding scores for your first 3 paid bounties. Shipping has no limit.
  • The Mergit team's wallets are not on the board.
  • The season closes on Nov 28, 23:59 UTC. Payments after that do not count for Season 0.
